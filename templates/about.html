About TESRECO Technologies

TESRECO Technologies is a software solutions provider specializing in IT consultancy, research, and talent development. This Intern Management System is developed as part of an academic-level evaluation program to demonstrate full-stack Python development.

Internship Objectives

The objective of this portal is to digitize training workflows including registration, tracking daily attendance logs, assigning internal mentors, and conducting performance evaluation reviews.

Mission and Vision

Mission: To streamline talent training workflows through simple web architectures.
Vision: To build simple, scalable tools that ease administrative management tasks in corporate environments.
