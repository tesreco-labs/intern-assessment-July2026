About TESRECO

Workforce development for industry-ready IT learning

Organization Overview

TESRECO is a workforce development organisation based in Indore, committed to bridging the gap between academic learning and industry expectations. TESRECO specializes in delivering practical, job-oriented IT Ready programs that prepare students and professionals for real world challenges.

Mission and Vision

Mission: deliver practical IT learning that helps interns become industry ready.

Vision: prepare students and professionals for real world technology challenges.
